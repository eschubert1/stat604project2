# Appendix A: Per-Treatment Mean Differences (ANCg, 20 Genes)

This appendix collects per-treatment mean difference plots for the 20 ANCg genes used in the matching analysis. Each figure shows the mean of pairwise differences (older − younger) aggregated per treatment across matched pairs.

Notes for reading:
- Positive values indicate higher expression in the older group; negative values indicate higher expression in the younger group.
- Consider both the mean shift and variability when comparing across genes.
